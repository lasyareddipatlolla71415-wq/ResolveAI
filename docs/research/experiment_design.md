# Experiment Design

Compare three systems:

1. Traditional ticket-first support.
2. RAG-only AI support.
3. ResolveAI adaptive support.

Ablations:

1. RAG only.
2. RAG plus historical outcomes.
3. RAG plus historical outcomes plus failure memory.
4. Full ResolveAI with risk and escalation policy.

Synthetic data must be clearly labeled and never presented as real-world evidence.
